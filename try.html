Our Story

A Legacy Built on
Integrity & Service

Nusrat International was founded with a singular purpose — to provide Bangladeshi families with safe, legal, and dignified pathways to opportunity abroad.

What began as a focused travel agency in the commercial heart of Banani, Dhaka has grown into one of Bangladesh's most trusted names across two critical sectors: international manpower recruitment and full-service travel solutions. Through more than 15 years of steady operation, the organization has maintained an unwavering commitment to ethical practice, regulatory compliance, and genuine care for every client.

Under the proprietorship of Alhaj Mohammad Zakir Hossain, Nusrat International holds a Government Recruiting Licence (RL-1429) and is a registered member of BAIRA — ensuring all overseas placements are conducted under the full protection of Bangladesh's national labour migration framework.

Its sister concern, Nusrat Travels, operates as a Government Approved Travel Agency, holding both ATAB membership and IATA accreditation — authorizing direct airline ticket issuance for all major international carriers, a credential that reflects a high standard of professional trust and financial standing within the industry.

"Our mission has always been simple: to serve our clients as if they were our own family — with honesty, care, and full accountability." — Alhaj Mohammad Zakir Hossain, Proprietor

Leadership

The Founder Behind
The Organisation

Alhaj Mohammad Zakir Hossain is the founder and sole proprietor of both Nusrat International and Nusrat Travels. A veteran of Bangladesh's travel and international recruitment industry, he has built both organisations on a foundation of regulatory compliance, professional integrity, and deep personal dedication.

His decision to pursue and actively maintain memberships in BAIRA, ATAB, and IATA accreditation reflects a belief — held from the organisation's very first day — that legitimacy and transparency are not optional attributes in a business that deals with people's livelihoods and spiritual aspirations.

Having performed Hajj himself, Alhaj Hossain brings a personal and deeply meaningful understanding to the Hajj and Umrah journeys his company facilitates each year for thousands of Bangladeshi pilgrims. This lived experience shapes the care and attention that defines every package Nusrat International offers.

"Every client who walks through our door is someone's father, mother, child. We carry that responsibility with the utmost seriousness — and with gratitude for their trust."

— Alhaj Mohammad Zakir Hossain

Company History

Key Milestones
Since 2010

2010

Company Founded

Nusrat International established at Banani C/A, Dhaka — beginning operations as a travel and manpower recruitment agency under the proprietorship of Alhaj Mohammad Zakir Hossain.

2012

BAIRA Registration

Formally registered with the Bangladesh Association of International Recruiting Agencies (BAIRA), cementing the agency's commitment to ethical overseas employment under the national framework.

2014

IATA Accreditation

Nusrat Travels received full IATA accreditation, authorising the direct issuance of international airline tickets — a milestone marking the agency's standing as a premier travel service provider in Bangladesh.

Launched structured Hajj and Umrah packages for Bangladeshi pilgrims, offering guided spiritual journeys with full visa processing, accommodation, and ground transportation.

Reached the milestone of 5,000 verified overseas job placements — workers deployed legally to the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Europe under full BAIRA compliance.
